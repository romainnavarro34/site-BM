We don't just make pizza.
We make your evening.

Born in a small kitchen, grown into a city staple. Quick'Pizza started with one oven, two riders, and a mad promise: deliver better pizza faster than anyone else.

The story

It started in 2018 with a simple question: why does delivery pizza always arrive lukewarm in a soggy box? Marco — our founder — spent six months perfecting a dough that travels better, a box that traps heat, and a routing algorithm that shaves minutes off every delivery.

Today we serve over 5,000 happy customers across the city, but the obsession hasn't changed: freshest ingredients, fastest delivery, best pizza you've ever had at home.

🍕

Our promise

We guarantee your pizza arrives within 30 minutes of ordering. If we miss that window — for any reason — your next order is completely free. No small print. No exceptions.

Every pizza is made fresh to order. We never pre-make, never freeze, never compromise. That's the Quick'Pizza standard.
